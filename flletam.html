The Problem

India's Freight Runs on
Broken Systems

Small transport businesses manage crore-rupee operations on WhatsApp, paper registers, and guesswork — costing time, money, and growth every single day.

📋

Manual Everything

Dispatchers coordinate loads via phone calls and handwritten logs. Errors are daily. Hours are wasted. There's no real record system in place.

📍

Zero Shipment Visibility

Owners have no live view of their vehicles or loads. Customers call endlessly for updates. Disputes happen with no proof trail to resolve them.

Billing Chaos

Invoices done in Excel or on paper. Payments tracked in notebooks. Cash flow is a mystery — until it's too late to act on the information.
